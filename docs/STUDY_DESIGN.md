# Study design and responsible reporting

## Constructs

- AI usage constraints
- AI frustration
- Tool switching
- Prompting skill
- Perceived learning effectiveness

## Minimum reproducibility release

A future reproducibility package should contain:

- an anonymized codebook and construct definitions;
- exact item wording and response scales, where consent permits;
- inclusion and exclusion rules;
- missing-data procedures;
- measurement-model settings;
- bootstrap seed and resampling configuration;
- structural-model specification;
- reliability, validity, HTMT, path, indirect-effect, and predictive tables; and
- a privacy statement describing what cannot be shared.

## Reporting cautions

- Cross-sectional associations do not establish causality.
- High construct overlap weakens discriminant validity.
- Self-report and common-method bias remain plausible.
- Premium status was not randomized and may reflect confounding factors.
- High in-sample R-squared values must be interpreted alongside benchmark prediction and validity checks.
